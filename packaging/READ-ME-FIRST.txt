CV Drafter: tailored CVs and cover letters, written only from facts about you
==========================================================================

1. Drag "CV Drafter" into your Applications folder (optional, but tidy).

2. Double-click CV Drafter.

   The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app for malicious software
   (it isn't from the App Store). That's expected. To allow it, once:
     - Click "Done" (or "OK") in that message.
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
       "Open Anyway" next to CV Drafter. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   (On older macOS: right-click CV Drafter > Open > Open.)

3. Your browser opens. The first start sets everything up (about 2 minutes,
   it needs internet). After that, it starts in seconds.

4. In the app:
   - Connect AI: click "Install Claude", then "Sign in with Claude" and log in
     with your Claude Pro or Max account. No API key needed.
   - Your profile: drop your CV into the chat. Then add anything else
     (reference letters, certificates, or just type extra facts).
   - Apply: paste a job posting. Done.

Your data stays on your Mac (in ~/Library/Application Support/CV Drafter).
To stop the app, click "Quit CV Drafter" at the bottom of the left menu.
To update, replace the app with a newer download; your data is kept.
